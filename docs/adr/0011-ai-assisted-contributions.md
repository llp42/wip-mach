# AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted contributions (partially or fully generated by an LLM) are
allowed. The following conditions apply:

- **Tooling legal compatibility**: the terms of the generative AI tool
  must not impose contractual restrictions that conflict with the
  distribution, modification, or use of its output in this project.
- **Licensing and attribution**: if the output includes pre-existing
  copyrighted material authored or owned by third parties, verify you
  may submit it under the relevant license before contributing it. The
  SPDX headers required by ADR 0010 stay the contributor's job.
- **Accountability**: the committer is accountable for the whole
  contribution — technical merit, security, license compliance, and
  utility. Understand every change and be prepared to justify it in
  review.
- **Disclosure**: when a significant portion of a contribution is
  generated or substantially assisted by a tool, disclosing the tool
  is encouraged. One convenient option for commits is a Git trailer
  such as `Assisted-By:`.
- **Bulk or automated changes**: discuss the intention before
  submitting bulk or autonomously generated contributions. Any such
  process is overseen by a human accountable for its behavior and
  output.

## Consequences

- An LLM-assisted change is not a new category of review: the same
  bars apply, and the committer owns them.
- `Signed-off-by` and `Assisted-By` trailers are the human's to
  write — the human runs `git commit` and owns the result.
